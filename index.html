Redact screenshots, PDFs and text — questions

How do I redact a screenshot?

Open redactly.fyi and paste the screenshot (Ctrl+V or ⌘V), drop it on the page, or tap “Choose a photo or PDF” on a phone. Emails, phone numbers, card numbers, API keys and other sensitive text are found and highlighted. Tap a highlight to keep it visible, drag over anything that was missed, then copy, share or save the image. The result has solid black boxes.

How do I redact a PDF for free?

Drop the PDF on redactly.fyi, check the highlighted items on every page, and download the redacted PDF. Each page is rebuilt from pixels, so text under a box can’t be copied out, and metadata such as author and title is removed. It’s free and needs no account.

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. Redactly runs entirely in your browser: text recognition and detection happen on your device, and the page is blocked from connecting to other servers. After the first visit it even works offline. The code is open source, so anyone can check.

Why not just blur or pixelate sensitive info?

Blurred and pixelated text can often be reconstructed. Redactly only uses solid, opaque boxes, and re-encodes the image so nothing of the original survives under them.

What does Redactly detect automatically?

Email addresses, phone numbers, credit card numbers, IBANs, US Social Security numbers, IP addresses, passwords, JWTs and API keys from AWS, GitHub, GitLab, Slack, Stripe, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and more. Names and addresses have no fixed format: draw a box over one once and Redactly offers to hide it everywhere it appears.

Can I redact text before pasting it into ChatGPT or another AI?

Yes. Paste the text and copy the safe version: sensitive values become placeholders like [EMAIL_1]. When the AI replies, paste the reply back and Redactly puts your real details back in.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Open redactly.fyi in any mobile browser, pick a photo or PDF, and share the result. You can add it to your home screen, and on Android you can share a photo straight to Redactly.

Can my company use or self-host Redactly?

Yes. Redactly is MIT-licensed and is just static files, so it can be hosted on an internal server with no backend. Files never leave the device.
